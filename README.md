Neon Bay is an original top-down open-world crime game: Rook, a getaway driver new in a sun-soaked coastal city, steals cars, outruns the police through a five-star wanted level, and runs five story jobs for fixer Delia Voss to pay off a $1,000 debt. It has a living city with traffic and pedestrians, drivable cars with damage, a minimap HUD, missions with payouts, a paint shop that clears your wanted level, saves, and fully synthesised sound — all in plain Canvas 2D with no libraries and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD/arrows to move and drive, Shift to run, Space to punch (handbrake in a car), E to enter or exit a car, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart. Pick a job with 1–5 on the title screen, then Enter to start.
